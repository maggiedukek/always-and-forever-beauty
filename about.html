My Story

Passion for brides.
Rooted in Minnesota.

Hi — I'm Colleen. I've been doing hair and makeup for over 20 years, and bridal work is truly where my heart is. There's something so special about being part of a couple's most important day, and I take that trust seriously.

Based in Pequot Lakes, MN, I serve brides throughout the Brainerd Lakes area and beyond. Whether your wedding is at a lakeside resort, a rustic barn, or a ballroom, I come to you — bringing everything needed for a relaxed, beautiful getting-ready morning.

My approach is always personal. I listen to what you want, bring my years of expertise to the table, and together we create a look that feels authentically you — just on your most radiant day.
