Booking & Travel Notes

A few little details so everything feels smooth and stress-free on your big day.

  • A signed contract and a $150 non-refundable retainer are required to hold your wedding date.
  • Travel is $0.75/mile from Pequot Lakes, MN. For venues over 2 hours away, $200 toward private lodging applies.
  • An early-start fee of $50/hour applies for any start before 6:00 AM.
  • Trials are optional and booked on weekdays in Pequot Lakes or Brainerd, MN — separate from the wedding day.
  • Final headcount and any changes are due 30 days before your wedding date.
  • I take a limited number of weddings each season — reach out early to save your date.
