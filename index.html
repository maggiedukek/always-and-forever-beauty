20+ years making brides feel like themselves

Hi, I'm Colleen Mulholland — a bridal hair and makeup artist based in Pequot Lakes, MN. I've spent over two decades perfecting my craft, and bridal work is where my heart has always been.

I believe your wedding morning should feel joyful, calm, and completely yours. I come to you, bring everything needed, and make sure every look is beautiful, long-lasting, and true to who you are.
